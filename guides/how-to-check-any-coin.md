# How to Check Any Coin in 10 Minutes (Beginner Guide)

You don’t need to be an expert. Just follow these 4 steps.

### Step 1 — Ask an AI to explain it simply (2 min)
Open ChatGPT, Claude or Grok and paste:

“Explain [coin name] like I’m a complete beginner. What is it, why does it exist, and what are the biggest risks? Maximum 8 sentences.”

Read the answer. If you still don’t understand — ask again: “Explain even simpler.”

### Step 2 — Look at basic numbers (2 min)
Go to CoinGecko → search the coin.

Look at:
- Current price
- Market Cap (how big it is)
- 24h volume (are people trading it?)
- All-time high (how far it fell from the top)

### Step 3 — Check if the project is alive (3 min)
Go to DeFiLlama (if it’s a DeFi project) or just search “[coin] TVL”.

Also open the project’s official website and Twitter/X.  
Ask yourself: “Does this look active or abandoned?”

### Step 4 — Ask about risks again (3 min)
Back to ChatGPT/Claude:

“Make a simple risk checklist for [coin]. Include: who controls the project, token unlock schedule, liquidity, and team reputation.”

If you see many red flags — skip this coin for now.

---

**Remember:**  
These tools help you understand faster.  
They do not tell you what to buy.  
Always start with small amounts you can afford to lose.
