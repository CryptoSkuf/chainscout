# How to Talk to AI About Crypto (Beginner Guide)

AI is only as good as the question you ask.  
Here are simple rules and ready prompts.

### Rule 1 — Always say you are a beginner
Add this to every question:  
“Explain like I’m a complete beginner.”

### Rule 2 — Ask for risks
Never ask only “Is this coin good?”.  
Always add: “What are the main risks?”

### Rule 3 — Ask for a checklist
Good ending for almost any question:  
“Give me a short checklist of what I should check next.”

---

### Ready prompts you can copy

**Understand a new coin**
```
Explain [coin name] like I’m a complete beginner. 
What is it, why does it exist, and what are the 3 biggest risks? 
Maximum 8 sentences.
```

**Check risks before buying**
```
Make a simple risk checklist for [coin name]. 
Include: who controls the project, token unlocks, liquidity, and team reputation.
```

**What is happening right now**
```
What are people saying about [coin name] in crypto right now? 
Keep it short and simple for a beginner.
```

**Compare two coins**
```
Compare [coin A] and [coin B] for a beginner. 
Which one is easier to understand and what are the main differences in risk?
```

---

**Remember:**  
AI can be wrong.  
Always check the same information on CoinGecko or the project website.
