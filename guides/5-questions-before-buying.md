# 5 Questions You Must Ask AI Before Buying Any Coin

Copy these questions one by one into ChatGPT, Claude or Grok.

### 1. What is this project actually doing?
“Explain what [coin name] does in simple words. Who uses it and why?”

### 2. Who controls it?
“Who has the power to change the rules or take the money in [coin name]? Is it decentralized or controlled by a small team?”

### 3. When do tokens unlock?
“Does [coin name] have token unlocks coming soon? How much supply will be released in the next 3–6 months?”

### 4. Is there real money and activity?
“Look at the trading volume and Total Value Locked (TVL) of [coin name]. Does it look active or almost dead?”

### 5. What are the biggest risks right now?
“List the top 5 risks of buying [coin name] right now for a complete beginner. Be honest and simple.”

---

**Tip:**  
If the AI answers are confusing or full of red flags — skip the coin.  
There will always be another one.
